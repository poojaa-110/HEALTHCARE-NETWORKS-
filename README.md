This analysis evaluates 54,966 cleaned patient records across clinical conditions, demographics, treatment billing, 
admission patterns, insurance coverage, medication distribution, length of stay and test-result status. The findings 
indicate a highly balanced patient mix across major categories, relatively stable average treatment costs over time, and 
a broadly even split between elective, urgent and emergency admissions.
